Transportation Choices

Image courtesy of Taxis lined up in New York

By Taxi/Rental

Taxis are available in Taniti City, and rental cars can be rented from a local rental agency near the airport.

Image courtesy of A yellow bus on the road

By Public Transportation

Public buses serve Taniti City and run from 5 a.m. to 11 p.m. every day. Private buses serve the rest of the island

Image courtesy of businessman riding bicycle on bridge in city

By Bike

Bikes and helmets are available to rent from several vendors (helmets are required by law).

Photo courtesy of JourneyWoman.

By Walking

Taniti City is fairly flat and very walkable. Many tourists stay in the area surrounding Merriton Landing: this area is easy to explore on foot.

Taniti is a small Pacific island under 500 square miles, featuring diverse terrain with beaches, a harbor, rainforests, and a mountainous interior with an active volcano. Its 20,000 residents traditionally relied on fishing and agriculture, but tourism has recently boosted the economy.
